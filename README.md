### Provincial Government of Nueva Vizcaya

I build web applications at the Provincial Information Technology Division (PITD).

- **LOGIS — Logistics, Order, and Goods Inventory System.** I built LOGIS at PITD for the Provincial General Services Office (PGSO). It manages purchase orders, bodega deliveries, received stock, open balances, audit history, and pickup notices.
- **IRIS — ICT Resources Inventory System.** I built IRIS at PITD to digitize ICT inventory sheets for devices, accountable personnel, installed software, and sign-offs. It supports guided entry, audited corrections, licensing and subscription reports, and Excel exports.

Both systems use Next.js, NestJS, TypeScript, Prisma, and Microsoft SQL Server.

### Earlier projects

- **OPIS — Online Provincial Inventory System.** Led the thesis team and full-stack development for PLGU Nueva Vizcaya. The system supports office-scoped access, realtime updates, requisitions, reporting, and Google Drive backups.
- **OASIS — Office Asset Services Inventory System.** Developed a system for inventory, asset deployment, and notifications during my DICT Nueva Vizcaya internship.
- **Automated Programmable School Bell System.** Programmed a Raspberry Pi school bell controller with a Python and Tkinter interface, relay control, and daily and emergency schedules.

### Education and experience

- **Nueva Vizcaya State University, Bambang Campus.** BS Computer Engineering, September 2022–July 2026.
- **DICT Nueva Vizcaya Provincial Office.** 400-hour internship, March–May 2026. Developed OASIS, deployed Starlink, and provided technical support.
- **NVSU Management Information System, Bambang Campus.** 350-hour internship, January–April 2026. Worked on campus networking, CCTV, and audiovisual support.
- **Credentials.** Career Service Professional Eligibility, CompTIA IT Fundamentals+, Safety Officer 1, and Cisco Networking Academy coursework.
